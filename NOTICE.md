## The script itself

**Foraxx Palette Studio** was written by **Nicolas Godingen**, who built it on the

## Dynamic combination maths and signature curves

**Foraxx Palette Utility** — Paul Hancock, © 2023–2024.

The dynamic combination expressions and the signature curves derive from this
script, which itself implements the dynamic PixelMath expressions published by
**Bill Blanshan** at
<https://thecoldestnights.com/2020/06/pixinsight-dynamic-narrowband-combinations-with-pixelmath/>.

At default settings in *Foraxx — dynamic SHO*, the starless output of this
script is bit-for-bit identical to the original's.

## Channel normalization

Follows the published method of the **NarrowbandNormalization** process by
**Mike Cranfield**, implemented from its documentation rather than from the
module itself.
